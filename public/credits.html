Photographs

Each place's photo credit links to its original source. The detail panel gives the image title, photographer and license. Photos are cropped using CSS for display; the original image file is otherwise unchanged. When a photo is adapted, any contribution to that adaptation is offered under the photo's stated license, including ShareAlike where applicable. License terms and any additional notices on the original file page apply.

Complete bundled photograph credits cover the hero, cards, itinerary thumbnails and saved trips. Newly fetched photograph credits include the same source and license fields. A source credit alone is not permission: images without supported reuse evidence are excluded from public delivery. These records do not warrant ownership or clear all non-copyright rights.

AI-generated atmosphere images

Places without an available verified photograph use visibly labeled, photorealistic AI-generated scenic images. These are fictional scenes, not photographs or reconstructions of the named landmark. They carry no claimed Creative Commons photo license. Image provenance and disclosures.

  • GeoNames: country, city and geographic records under CC BY 4.0. Changes include filtering, destination matching and generated display descriptions.
  • © OpenStreetMap contributors: map and place data under ODbL 1.0. Changes include filtering, matching to cities and field normalization. ODbL-derived database contributions remain under ODbL. The adapted ODbL component, mapped-place extract and Sa Pa extract are available with their source records and separate per-photo notices.
