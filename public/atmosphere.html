About AI atmosphere images

When a verified location photograph is unavailable, Roam displays an AI-generated scenic atmosphere image. These are fictional scenes chosen by the place's theme, not photographs, reconstructions or evidence of conditions at the named destination. Images are visibly labeled in discovery cards and place details; thumbnail descriptions identify generated imagery too.
