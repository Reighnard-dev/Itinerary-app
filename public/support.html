Support & feedback

Need help planning, found a problem, or have a suggestion? Email roamtravelsupport30@gmail.com. ROAM Support handles help, privacy, and copyright requests.

Account deletion or data copy

Email from your registered account address with the subject “Account deletion” or “Data copy”. We will verify ownership before acting. Never include your password. You can also delete individual trips in My itineraries and export them from the planner.

Report a problem

Include your device/browser, destination, what you tried, and what happened. Avoid sending sensitive itinerary details unless needed to reproduce the problem.

Copyright reports

Include the exact Roam image or page URL, a link identifying the original work, why you hold or represent the relevant rights, and how we can contact you. We will investigate and remove or correct material where appropriate.

Help us improve

Tell us whether you could find a destination, add places to days, save and reopen your trip, and export it. Let us know what stopped you or would make planning easier.

Coverage is strongest in established guide destinations such as Kyoto, Tokyo, Paris, and Sa Pa. Other cities can have fewer listings. Always confirm hours, prices, reservations, and access with the venue.
