Privacy notice

Updated 23 November 2026. This notice describes Roam's current account and travel-planning features. Contact ROAM Support at roamtravelsupport30@gmail.com.

Information we use

When you create an account, we process your email address, optional display name, authentication credentials, and saved itineraries. Trips can contain destinations, dates, visit times, and notes you enter. Avoid putting passport numbers, payment details, or sensitive personal information in notes.

Guest drafts, account drafts, and favorites are stored in your browser. Signing out does not erase all browser storage. Clear site data on shared devices. Local preview accounts use the local server database; hosted accounts use Supabase authentication and database storage.

Why we process it

We use this information to authenticate you, store and retrieve your plans, respond to support requests, and protect the service against abuse. Server and hosting logs may contain IP addresses, request information, and error records.

Service providers and maps

Netlify hosts the public site; Supabase provides hosted authentication and account storage. Their processing may occur outside your country. Embedded maps contact OpenStreetMap or Google. Opening venue, guide, or map links shares your request with that provider under its own policies. A directions request can send your chosen origin and destination to Google. Current location is requested only when you select that option and grant browser permission.

Cookies and browser storage

Local preview uses an essential session cookie. Cloud authentication stores session information in your browser. Roam also stores drafts and favorites locally. The application does not include an advertising or analytics SDK; hosting and external map providers can still process technical usage data.

Retention and your choices

Saved trips remain until you delete them or request account deletion. Browser drafts remain until you clear them or clear site data. Provider logs and backups follow provider retention settings; deletion from active storage may not immediately remove backup copies. We do not promise a backup purge time we cannot verify.

You can delete individual trips in My itineraries and export them from the planner. To request an account data copy, correction, account deletion, or ask a privacy question, email support from the account's registered email address. We verify ownership before acting. Do not send your password. Your rights and our obligations depend on applicable law.

Security and updates

Access controls protect saved account data. No service can guarantee absolute security. This notice will be updated when the service or its data practices change.
