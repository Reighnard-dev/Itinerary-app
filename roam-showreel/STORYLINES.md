# Storylines — Roam Showreel (25 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

Selected: **Option A — "An Entire World, Organized"**

## Option A — "An Entire World, Organized" (Selected)
- **Device & form**: Object-to-object morph chain + scenic parallax world shift.
- **What you see and feel**: Bright, kinetic Apple-grade craft where every visual transforms directly into the next — a spinning globe morphs into Roam's yellow compass badge, a cursor clicks it into Roam's real search bar, real Kyoto discovery cards snap into a day-by-day itinerary, and the film opens up into a golden-hour Kyoto panoramic shot with floating frosted Roam trip cards before a snappy 3-word closer and full-bleed Sunshine Yellow lockup.
- **First 2 seconds**: Crisp `#FAF5EA` canvas with subtle topographic contour lines; `"The world is"` anchored in Manrope ExtraBold as `"everywhere."` whips in with directional motion blur and a warm gold highlight sweep, pushing straight into the chaos-vs-globe beat.
- **Beats**: Kinetic hook + Globe → `roam.` yellow bloom & cursor click → Morph into real Search Bar (`Kyoto, Japan · 5 days`) → Real Discover screen + depth-of-field popping Place Cards (`Iconic spot` / `Hidden gem`) → Real-photo mosaic framing `"See your whole adventure."` → Contrast Charcoal world flip with real Day-by-Day Planner & Interactive Route Map → Generated golden-hour Kyoto vista with floating frosted Roam UI widgets → Staccato `"Discover it. / Plan it. / Roam it."` → Full-bleed `#F5CF43` flood with real `R` icon + `roam.` + `"A little less ordinary."`
- **Look**: Warm `#FAF5EA` parchment editorial canvas, `#1A1D18` deep charcoal contrast cut, `#F5CF43` Sunshine Yellow focal accent, Manrope ExtraBold display & DM Sans body, topographic elevation contours from `TravelBackground.tsx`.
- **Sound**: Crisp upbeat CC0 indie-electronic pulse ducked cleanly under a natural female voiceover, paired with frame-accurate cursor clicks, directional whooshes, pops, and a riser into the finale.

## Option B — "Is It Possible?"
- **Device & form**: Question-and-morph pill opener + cut-on-the-beat dark/light contrast flips.
- **What you see and feel**: High-contrast charcoal/parchment typography showreel where a planning pill squashes and turns yellow into `"1 trip."`, cutting rapidly through punch-ins on Roam's 250 countries, 34,240 cities, one-click `Suggest Day 1`, and drag-to-reorder stops.
- **First 2 seconds**: Light `#FAF5EA` ground `"Is it possible"` snaps to `#191B17` charcoal with a morphing pill transforming `"47 open tabs"` into `"1 trip."`
- **Beats**: Question hook → Morphing pill → Discover desktop punch-in → Depth-of-field widget cascade → Highlighter sweep → Real Planner + Map → Destination burst → Charcoal `R` + `roam.` end card.
- **Look**: Strict 3-tone Parchment (`#FAF5EA`), Charcoal (`#191B17`), Sunshine (`#F5CF43`).
- **Sound**: Tight minimal rhythmic pulse with mechanical snaps, whip-whooshes, and female VO.

## Option C — "The Scenic Route"
- **Device & form**: Continuous camera flight across Roam's topographic contour map world.
- **What you see and feel**: One gliding camera sweeps along Roam's dashed golden explorer trail over `#FAF5EA` contours and sage mountain ridges (`#ACC1A7`); each waypoint pin opens into a real Roam screen, postcard stack, or golden-hour sunrise vista.
- **First 2 seconds**: Camera races along the dashed trail and slams onto Roam's `"TAKE THE SCENIC ROUTE"` passport stamp, irising open onto Kyoto photography.
- **Beats**: Trail-to-stamp iris → Photo plate flight → Waypoint dive into Discover → Trail zoom into Planner & Directions map → Lift into Kyoto sunrise vista with floating cards → Pull-back to `R` + `roam.` end card.
- **Look**: Travel-journal parchment `#FAF5EA`, sage ridges `#ACC1A7`, `#262923` ink, `#F5CF43` route accents.
- **Sound**: Warm cinematic travel groove, atmospheric swell, map-pin pops, and female VO.
